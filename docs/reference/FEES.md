# Fees

## Launch fee

The PONS creation page currently displays a launch fee of `0.0005 ETH` on Robinhood Chain.

## Creator fees

The interface states that the creator wallet receives creator fees but does not publish a universal percentage, calculation method, or distribution schedule in the reviewed pages.

## Trading fees

Trades occur through a Uniswap market. The exact Uniswap version and pool fee tier are not published in the reviewed launch form and must be read from verified on-chain contracts.

## Other costs

- Robinhood Chain gas.
- Developer buy amount, when selected.
- Uniswap price impact and pool trading fees.
- Slippage between quote and execution.
- ERC-20 approval and wrapping/unwrapping costs where applicable.

Display these separately. Do not label gas, price impact, or developer buy principal as a PONS protocol fee.
